## Descripción completa (máx. 4000)
Sacred Texts is a lexical analysis tool for the comparative study of religious texts in the public domain.

It computes normalised frequencies, concordances and vocabulary distribution using standard corpus linguistics methods. It does not interpret doctrine.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Measure how often a term occurs in each corpus, normalised per 10,000 words
• Group concepts: search for "mercy" and also find "compassion" and "clemency"
• See where a term clusters within each work, book by book
• Inspect every occurrence with its exact canonical reference
• Read passages side by side, one column per tradition
• Search by meaning, not only by word

TEXTS INCLUDED
All editions are in the public domain: King James Bible (1611), Reina-Valera (1909), JPS Tanakh (1917), the Quran in Palmer (1880) and Pickthall (1930) translations, and the Bhagavad Gita in Arnold (1885) and Sivananda translations.

METHOD
Frequencies are never shown raw: the corpora differ greatly in size and direct comparison would mislead. Differences are tested with log-likelihood, the standard in corpus linguistics.

Each text is a specific translation, and the app says so: the figures describe the translation, not the original.

Available in Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, Arabic and Hindi.
